# Metamagic

Read when extending metamagic grants, application choices, or connecting them to casting. Runtime spell preparation is documented in [spells.md](spells.md).

## Current implementation

- `Spellcasting` owns initially empty `spellcasting.<id>.preparationMetamagicGrants` and `castingMetamagicGrants` collections. A grant in one never implies permission in the other; classes/items/feats still do not populate them automatically.
- The initial closed vocabulary contains the nine core feats plus Elemental, Reach, Intensified and Selective, not the entire Pathfinder catalog. Applications hold an enum, normalized grant ID and typed options (Heighten target level, Reach steps, Elemental energy/mode). Grant energy restricts Elemental choices.
- `prepare(slot(s), spellId, applications = {})` validates grants, spell applicability, distinct metamagic types, combined slot costs and the existing preparation rules. Linked slots must resolve the same base spell level. Operational checks receive the effective level, including Heighten. The selected applications are stored in slot state, Data and View.
- `consume(slot, replacementSpellId = nullopt, applications = {})` accepts casting additions and returns a `MetamagicEvaluation`: spell ID, base/effective/required-slot/nominal-slot levels and combined applications. Ignoring the return value preserves existing calls. Ordinary consumption combines the prepared snapshot with the additions; conversion evaluates only the replacement's applications. Casting additions do not overwrite prepared Data/View or become a saved cast log.
- `MetamagicRegistry::evaluate()` is a pure query; `resolve()` remains a lower-level grant lookup, not complete authorization. Evaluation enforces conversion-only scope, duplicate types across both phases, one rod, and rod level caps against required/effective level. Standard versus waived cost is honored; nominal cost is retained. The caller checks the available slot and level-9 ceiling. Higher chosen slots do not automatically increase effective level.
- Applicability uses structured duration/range/component/area/casting-time metadata. Empower, Maximize, Elemental, Intensified and Widen require the grant's explicit `isApplicable(spell, application)` predicate because damage expressions and area shape are not structurally modeled. Extend with Other duration and Quicken with Round/Special casting time also need that predicate. Round alone does not say how many rounds: a provider may confirm the permitted one-round case. Missing evidence rejects rather than interpreting prose. Provider callbacks may impose additional class/source restrictions (including Bard Silent restrictions), must be pure, and must not mutate pools/grants/state.
- The schema cannot yet evaluate metamagic interactions that transform applicability (e.g. one metamagic changing metadata used by another). Applicability is checked against the base definition. Damage, range, component and casting-time effects are not applied to actual gameplay; the returned applications describe what a future cast resolver must apply.
- `MetamagicUsageCost` now carries a shared handle to a source-owned `MetamagicUsagePool` as well as pool ID and positive cost. The source creates/reuses this pool across grants/casting instances. IDs must match; ambiguous distinct handles with the same ID in one payment are rejected. Missing handles fail at payment, so metadata registration cannot grant free uses. There is no global pool service or item inventory integration yet; providers must reuse the authoritative handle for a source.
- `pay()` aggregates all costs per shared pool, verifies all balances and identities before any deduction, and commits without callbacks/allocation. Prepare stages its new map before payment, then swaps; consume completes queries before payment and then only changes slot state. Failure leaves slots and every pool unchanged. Predicates are read-only and execution is single-threaded; no synchronization is provided.
- Preparation-phase limited uses are paid on successful preparation only. Casting-phase uses are paid on each successful consumption, even when a consumption exemption preserves the slot. Ordinary casting does not re-pay or re-resolve earlier prepared applications. Existing preparations retain their calculated snapshot when a grant changes or is removed.
- `load()` reconstructs and validates applications using current preparation grants/costs without paying/checking remaining uses. Reconstruct grant configuration first. This matches the existing strict loading model: an absent grant/incompatible selection fails atomically. Consequently loading after grant removal may fail even though the still-live prepared snapshot can be consumed. Changing that persistence policy is separate work.
- Grant removal/re-registration, `clear()`, group replacement and `load()` never refill/refund source uses. Pool `reset(remaining)` is explicit and rejects negative counts; when to replenish is controlled by the player/source, not time/rest gating. Pool persistence belongs to its source and is not yet integrated with item/sheet JSON.
- `SelectiveMetamagicTargets` remains a separate cast-time value structure, not part of preparation and not yet accepted by consume. Target existence/count/area validation awaits the casting resolver.
- Tests cover choices/costs, phase isolation, empty permissions, source/rod constraints, lifecycle/rollback, preparation and casting integration, Heighten, duplicates, Data/View/load, source snapshots, one rod and caps, conversion, shared balances across casting instances, multiple-pool atomic failure, exhausted/missing pools, throwing predicates, and no refill on re-registration. Sheet suite: 64 passing; play tests excluded.

## Rules checked and design consequences

- [General metamagic](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Talenti_di_Metamagia): ordinary adjustments increase the required slot, not the spell's effective level; distinct metamagics add their costs and a feat cannot be applied twice to one spell. Applicability is feat-specific. Preparing and applying during spontaneous casting are separate cases; conversion may also allow casting-time application. Future evaluation must combine prepared and additional applications before checking duplicates. Spell-like abilities and activation of spell-containing items are not the same workflow.
- [Heighten Spell / Incantesimi Intensificati](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Intensificati): the target effective level is a choice, paid in addition to other metamagic increases. Merely using a higher slot does not heighten the spell. [Intensified Spell / Incantesimi Intensi](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Intensi) instead changes a damage-dice cap; these must remain distinct enum values.
- [Elemental Spell](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Elementali): energy is fixed by the acquired feat, while full replacement versus half damage is an application choice. [Reach Spell](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_con_Portata): cost depends on selected range steps, and applicability requires a supported initial range. [Selective Spell](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Selettivi): excluded targets are selected on casting, including for a previously prepared selective spell.
- [Metamagic rods](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Verghe#Verghe_Metamagiche): apply at casting without increasing the slot, grant no feat ownership, allow one rod per spell, and have grade limits. A rod can be combined with possessed feats. [Empower rod](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Verga_Metamagica_di_Potenziamento) has three uses per day; this belongs to the item's shared usage pool, not each casting instance.
- [Spell Perfection](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimo_Perfetto) demonstrates why nominal metamagic cost must remain available even when payment is waived, and why blanket cost subtraction cannot model all exceptions. This feature is not implemented by the `Waived` metadata alone.
- English terminology was cross-checked through Golarion's linked [Elemental Spell](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/metamagic-feats/elemental-spell-metamagic), [Reach Spell](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/metamagic-feats/reach-spell-metamagic), and [Rods](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic-items/rods). Linked Heighten/Selective pages failed to load; the Golarion rule text was available.

## Remaining work

Spontaneous casting calls the shared evaluator/payment through `consume(slot, spellId, applications)`. It uses casting-phase grants and rejects conversion-only scope. Its state is only slot consumption; metamagic stays in the transient result. Flexible casting reuses a prepared snapshot across casts, with separate casting slots. Combining prepared and additional metamagic requires an applicable mixing permission; paid permissions are explicitly selected and their costs join the atomic casting payment. No permission grants metamagic by itself, and neither mode repays preparation costs. See [spells.md](spells.md). Combined sheet suite: 66 passing; play tests excluded.

1. Add concrete class/feat/item providers and source pool persistence; keep catalog expansion separate.
2. Implement source-owned cost exceptions/reductions beyond the Standard/Waived policy. Spell Perfection needs its own eligibility/nominal-cost ceiling; the generic waiver does not implement that feat.
3. Add the casting resolver for effects, target selections and casting-time categories. Respect the player's timing authority; do not introduce rest/recovery gating.
4. Expand the closed metamagic vocabulary and handle interactions that change another application's applicability in focused steps.

Additional applicability references checked during integration: [Extend Spell](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Estesi) and [Quicken Spell](https://golarion.altervista.org/wiki/Incantesimi_Rapidi), including the FAQ permitting a one-round spell to be quickened.
