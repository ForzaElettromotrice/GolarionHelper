# Special Defenses

Read this document when changing informational defensive abilities or their Views.

- `CharacterSheet` owns informational `SpecialDefenses`, currently covering energy resistance, energy vulnerability, damage reduction, immunities, spell resistance, fast healing, and regeneration in `CharacterSheetView`; it does not alter `HitPoints::damage`, resolve incoming spells, advance time, or have independently persisted state. Components register immutable `EnergyResistance` grants through `specialDefense.energyResistance.grants`, reversible `EnergyResistanceAdjustment` entries through `specialDefense.energyResistance.adjustments`, immutable `EnergyVulnerability` grants through `specialDefense.energyVulnerability.grants`, immutable `DamageReduction` grants through `specialDefense.damageReduction.grants`, reversible numeric `DamageReductionAdjustment` entries through `specialDefense.damageReduction.adjustments`, immutable `Immunity` grants through `specialDefense.immunity.grants`, immutable `SpellResistance` grants through `specialDefense.spellResistance.grants`, reversible `SpellResistanceAdjustment` entries through `specialDefense.spellResistance.adjustments`, immutable `FastHealing` grants through `specialDefense.fastHealing.grants`, and immutable `Regeneration` grants through `specialDefense.regeneration.grants`.
- Each energy-resistance grant has stable identity, provenance, a `DamageType`, an expression-backed base value, and normalized selector tags. Physical and untyped damage are rejected; the model accepts the five ordinary energy types plus positive energy, negative energy, and force so exceptional canonical sources can be represented without a second damage enum. Multiple grants for one energy never sum: after adjustments, the highest effective grant determines the displayed resistance, with tied sources all identified as equivalent determinants.
- Energy-resistance adjustments only increase matching existing grants and never create resistance by themselves. Their selector may target an energy, one exact grant, any of a set of tags, and exclusions; this supports improvements to racial/class resistance and fallback-grant patterns without mutating another component's state. Within one semantic stacking group only the highest matching increase applies to a grant, while distinct groups add. Exact-grant dependencies prevent removal of the target until their adjustment is removed; broad selectors remain dormant and automatically apply when a matching grant appears. `SpecialDefensesView` exposes each energy's effective value, every grant's base/effective values and tags, all matching applied/suppressed adjustments, and why a lower grant or adjustment did not contribute.
- Energy vulnerabilities are immutable source-owned grants with stable identity, provenance, a typed energy, and optional applicability text. They accept the same nonphysical energy-capable `DamageType` values as energy resistance and reject physical or untyped damage. Grants are grouped by energy in `SpecialDefensesView`, retain every source without stacking, and expose the canonical 50% damage increase. They remain informational and do not alter incoming damage. Vulnerabilities to non-energy effects belong in the existing conditional Modifier pipeline, while source-specific exceptional consequences remain owned by their component or a `Reminder`.
- Each damage-reduction grant has stable identity, provenance, an expression-backed nonnegative base value, optional applicability text, and normalized tags. Its bypass is represented as OR-of-AND alternatives: separate alternatives model rules such as `argento o bene`, multiple traits in one alternative model `argento e bene`, and no alternatives represents RD/—. Bypass traits cover physical damage types, magic/epic weapons, special materials, and alignments, with Italian display names. The View deliberately exposes every grant rather than one universal total because which RD prevails depends on the incoming attack.
- Damage-reduction adjustments select an existing grant by exact ID, any matching tag, and exclusions, then apply a signed expression-backed delta without changing its bypass or applicability. They never create RD. Within one semantic stacking group the strongest increase or strongest reduction applies; opposite nonzero signs in one group are rejected as ambiguous, while distinct groups combine. Effective RD is clamped to zero. Exact-grant dependencies prevent target removal, while broad selectors may remain dormant and reactivate when matching grants appear. The View exposes base/effective values and every matching applied or suppressed adjustment with provenance and reason. Attack-specific multiplication/caps, exceptions to bypass equivalence, and resolving the best RD against an incoming attack deliberately remain source-owned Reminder text rather than model behavior because this sheet has no combat-resolution engine.
- Exceptional RD stacking is declared directly by the grant that owns the exception through normalized `stacksWithTags`; no separate endpoint or permission lifecycle exists. Two grants are compatible when either grant accepts one of the other's tags, but compatibility is not transitive. `SpecialDefensesView` resolves each grant's compatible IDs and calculates every maximal set whose grants are mutually pairwise compatible, using the grants' already adjusted effective values. Each combination exposes its maximum value when every component applies and the grant/tag provenance authorizing every pair. A combination is flattened only when every component has identical bypass and applicability; otherwise its grant components remain authoritative because different incoming attacks may bypass different portions.
- Immunities deliberately use an open semantic target ID plus an Italian display name instead of a closed enum: canonical rules may grant immunity to damage types, afflictions, conditions, effect families, individual spells, or broader trait bundles. Every grant retains its stable ID, source, and optional applicability text. Grants sharing a target are grouped in the View without stacking or collapsing their provenance, and the same target ID must always use the same display name. Immunities are exclusively informational: they do not automatically reject damage or suppress condition entries, including source-controlled conditions generated by the sheet. Immunities are source-owned runtime structure, so components recreate them after load.
- Each spell-resistance grant has stable identity, provenance, an expression-backed nonnegative base value, optional applicability text, a flag stating whether that source may be lowered voluntarily, and normalized selector tags. RI grants never stack. `SpecialDefensesView` exposes one general profile when an unconditional grant exists and a separate profile for every distinct contextual applicability; each contextual profile includes unconditional grants as fallback and selects its highest effective applicable grant. This supports fixed, level-scaled, arcane-only, school/descriptor/source-limited, and non-lowerable canonical RI without pretending that free-form applicability text can be evaluated automatically.
- Spell-resistance adjustments select existing grants by exact ID, any matching tag, and exclusions, then contribute a signed expression-backed delta without creating RI. An adjustment may itself have applicability text: unconditional adjustments affect every profile, while contextual adjustments create or affect only their matching profile. Within one semantic stacking group the strongest increase or strongest reduction applies, opposite nonzero signs are rejected as ambiguous, and final values are clamped to zero. Exact-grant dependencies prevent target removal while broad selectors may remain dormant. The View retains full applied/suppressed provenance per profile. Lowering RI, restoring it at the next turn, checking whether an incoming spell permits RI, and rolling the caster-level check remain outside this informational model.
- Each fast-healing grant has stable identity, provenance, an expression-backed nonnegative rate, optional applicability text, normalized tags, and a semantic stacking group. Within one context only the highest grant in each group contributes, while distinct groups add; unconditional grants are fallback sources in conditional contexts. `SpecialDefensesView` exposes every resolved source, the effective rate for each context, and why a lower same-group source is suppressed. The default group is non-stacking, while a canonical source that explicitly stacks must declare a distinct group.
- Each regeneration grant has stable identity, provenance, an expression-backed nonnegative rate, optional applicability text, normalized tags, and an optional free-form interruption description such as `Acido o fuoco`. The View preserves grants as independent profiles instead of calculating a universal total because value and interruption rules belong together. Fast healing and regeneration are source-owned runtime structure and never heal automatically, change death state, inspect incoming damage, or call `HitPoints`; their source components recreate them after load.
