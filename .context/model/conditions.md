# Conditions

Read this document when changing condition activation, derived conditions, condition effects, or canonical condition definitions.

- `CharacterSheet` owns `ConditionManager` and registers all 30 canonical character-condition definitions currently represented after every model component has installed its resources. The sheet exposes `addCondition`/`removeCondition` as the manual GUI channel; automatic component-owned entries still use `condition.entries`. `ConditionManager` owns definitions and active entries, validates references and duplicate IDs, and exposes active/count/origin queries plus View and persistence snapshots. Every definition has one or more ordered, uniquely identified stages, and every stage may declaratively derive other conditions at a positive severity and declare reversible effects. A definition may declare one required textual entry parameter: the View exposes its Italian label, every entry exposes its value, and manual values survive save/load; entries missing a required parameter or supplying one to an incompatible condition are rejected.
- Condition activation has two physically separate channels. `ConditionManager::addManualEntry` creates GUI/table-owned entries that only `removeManualEntry` may remove; additions through the `condition.entries` collection are automatically source-controlled and only collection removal may remove them. Callers cannot choose or spoof `ConditionEntryOrigin`; `Derived` is assigned only by the internal dependency resolver. `effectiveSeverity` resolves `Shared` conditions to 1 while active, sums `PerEntry` severity as a diagnostic total without collapsing the independently stored entries, and resolves `Escalating` conditions by summing independent sources plus the greatest contributor in each stacking group, then taking the greater result against every non-contributing minimum severity. Progressive calculation saturates directly at the final available stage, so no unused uncapped severity is retained or exposed.
- Derived conditions are reconciled transitively from all cumulatively active stages into deterministic internal entries with `Derived` origin. Reconciliation starts from manual and source-controlled entries, computes the complete desired closure in temporary state, and commits only on success; unknown active targets, ID conflicts, or invalid graphs therefore cannot leave partial state. Derived entries cannot be removed through either external channel, disappear with their originating stage, preserve independent target sources, and circular derivation graphs are rejected when the closing definition is registered.
- A stage effect has a stable ID, an Italian GUI description, and an apply callback that receives `ResourceManager` plus a stable effect-instance context and returns its exact cleanup callback. Shared and escalating effects exist once per active stage, while `PerEntry` effects have a separate instance for every entry. The manager diffs desired and applied instances, keeps unchanged effects alive, applies only newly reached stages, and cleans only stages or entries that disappeared. A failed application cleans every effect created by that attempted transition and leaves the entry state unchanged; cleanup callbacks have an explicit no-throw contract. Destroying the manager cleans remaining effects and unregisters its collection resource.
- `ConditionManagerView` exposes every definition in deterministic ID order, its resolved severity, ordered stages and activation state, declared derivations, effect descriptions and active effect instances, plus every active entry with provenance, stacking data, and origin. `ConditionManagerSaveData` contains only manual entries; source-controlled entries are recreated by their owning sheet components, derived entries are reconciled from active stages, and effects are reapplied from definitions. Loading all manual entries is one atomic manager transition.
- Every canonical definition has its own implementation file under `core/src/character/conditions`; shared effect factories create modifiers, contributions, replacements, suppressions, defense alternatives, and movement adjustments with exact reversible cleanup. All definition files are compiled and registered through `registerCanonicalConditions`. A smoke test activates and removes every definition, including the maximum stages of Paura and Affaticamento.
- Canonical conditions now register their non-automatable table consequences as reversible reminder effects. Static effects publish Italian messages while contextual effects can derive a message from the condition entry's source and parameter. Removing a condition stage or per-source entry removes its exact reminder; manual conditions reconstructed from SaveData recreate their reminders instead of persisting duplicate derived messages. Sanguinante is `PerEntry` so each independent parameterized source publishes its own damage reminder.
- `AbilityChecks` registers `abilityCheck.all` and one descendant for each ability. `AbilityView` exposes the calculated raw ability-check total and its modifiers separately from the ability score and modifier, so check penalties never alter the underlying score. Initiative combines its own hierarchy with the descendant for each displayed ability option, matching the option-specific skill protocol.
- `Strikes` registers `damage.weaponRoll` beneath `damage.all`. The weapon, natural, and unarmed damage categories inherit from it, while generic ranged, thrown, and projectile categories do not; a thrown weapon still inherits it through its simultaneous weapon tag. This lets effects modify weapon damage rolls once without affecting unrelated damage.
- The canonical `sickened`/`Infermo` definition is registered only after every sheet component has installed its resources. Its five reversible actions apply untyped penalties of 2 to `attack.all`, `damage.weaponRoll`, `savingThrow.all`, `skill.all`, and `abilityCheck.all`, covering attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks. Multiple sources still apply this package only once, and removing the last source cleans up every generated modifier.
- The canonical `fear`/`Paura` definition has three cumulative stages: `shaken`/`Scosso`, `frightened`/`Spaventato`, and `panicked`/`In preda al panico`. Scosso applies reversible penalties of 2 to attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks; the higher stages publish their forced-flight, random-direction, dropped-item, and cowering rules as reminders. Two ordinary Scosso sources escalate to Spaventato; a severity-2 source plus an independent Scosso source escalates to In preda al panico. Repeated applications from the same effect can share a `stackingGroup`, and exceptional effects can set `contributesToEscalation = false`.
- The canonical `fatigue`/`Affaticamento` definition has two cumulative stages. `fatigued`/`Affaticato` applies penalties of 2 to `str` and `dex`, inhibits Correre and Caricare, and reminds that further fatigue causes exhaustion and that 8 hours of total rest remove fatigue. `exhausted`/`Esausto` adds another 4 points of penalty to both abilities, a 50% `MovementAdjustment`, and its one-hour-rest reminder. Downgrading removes only the second-stage effects. Entries use the same severity, escalation, and stacking-key policy as Paura, including non-escalating fatigue sources.
- The canonical `dazzled`/`Abbagliato` definition applies a penalty of 1 to `attack.all` and a conditional penalty of 1 to `skill.perception` under the Italian condition label `Basate sulla vista`. Its Perception penalty is therefore absent from the permanent total, visible as a conditional result to the GUI, and included when that calculation context is active.
- The canonical `entangled`/`Intralciato` definition applies penalties of 2 to `attack.all` and 4 to `dex`, plus a 50% `MovementAdjustment` matching every movement grant, inhibits Correre and Caricare, and reminds the user of the spellcasting Concentration check. Every effect is reversible and removed with the final condition entry.
- The canonical `blinded`/`Accecato` definition applies a penalty of 2 to `armorClass.all`, registers an `ArmorClassAbilitySuppression`, and adds conditional penalties of 4 to `skillCheck.str`, `skillCheck.dex`, and opposed `skill.perception` checks. It publishes reminders for automatic sight-based failure, opponents' total concealment, and Acrobazia CD 10 when moving faster than half speed. Removing its final entry reverses every registered effect.
- The canonical `cowering`/`Accovacciato` definition applies a penalty of 2 to `armorClass.all`, registers an `ArmorClassAbilitySuppression`, and inhibits every action; all effects are removed with its final entry. `Paura` still describes becoming Accovacciato when trapped instead of creating the entry automatically, because the model cannot determine whether escape is possible.
- The canonical `fascinated`/`Affascinato` definition applies a conditional penalty of 4 to `skill.all` for checks required as reactions, inhibits every action, and publishes reminders for potential threats, obvious threats, and an ally shaking the creature free.
- The canonical `deafened`/`Assordato` definition applies a penalty of 4 to `initiative` and publishes reminders for automatic failure of sound-based Perception checks and the 20% failure chance for spells with verbal components.
- The canonical `staggered`/`Barcollante` definition inhibits every round-complete action while preserving standard, movement, free, swift, and immediate actions. Its reminder states the remaining per-round choice between one standard or one move action until turn-state tracking can enforce it. `HitPoints` creates and removes its own source-controlled entry when positive non-lethal damage equals current hit points.
- The canonical `confused`/`Confuso` definition has no automatic actions. Its per-turn percentile table, target selection, retaliation, beneficial touch, and attack-of-opportunity rules are published as reminders because resolving them depends on table and scene state outside the character sheet.
- The canonical `dazed`/`Frastornato` definition inhibits every action. Its usually one-round duration is owned by the applying source and later by `TimeManager`. It intentionally applies no armor-class penalty.
- The canonical `flatFooted`/`Impreparato` definition reversibly suppresses positive ability bonuses in armor-class calculation and positive Dexterity in CMD, and reminds that attacks of opportunity are unavailable absent an exception such as Riflessi in Combattimento.
- The canonical `grappled`/`In Lotta` definition reversibly applies a penalty of 4 to Dexterity and a penalty of 2 to `attack.all`. Because BMC inherits the attack hierarchy, it also installs a stacking generic +2 on the exact Lottare BMC resource; this cancels only the condition's inherited -2 for attempts to grapple or escape while preserving any reduction caused by the lower Dexterity when a replacement ability uses it. Actions tagged as physical movement are inhibited; attacks of opportunity, two-handed actions, and spellcasting restrictions are reminders until their semantic systems can enforce them.
- The canonical `disabled`/`Inabile` definition applies a reversible 50% movement adjustment to every mode and inhibits round-complete actions. It reminds the user of the one-standard-or-move limit and the lost hit point after strenuous actions. `HitPoints` owns its automatic source entry at every non-positive current value until its death threshold is reached, without attempting to infer stability or consciousness.
- The canonical `incorporeal`/`Incorporeo` definition deliberately has no provisional combat modifiers. Its incoming and outgoing damage rules, object traversal, prohibited physical maneuvers, and physical properties are exposed as reminders.
- The canonical `helpless`/`Indifeso` definition reversibly registers a final Dexterity replacement of 0. This produces modifier -5 and propagates through the existing `dex`/`dexMod` targets to armor class, CMD, Reflex, initiative, skills, and every other consumer. Melee attackers' +4, sneak-attack eligibility, and the complete coup-de-grace procedure are reminders because they describe incoming encounter interactions.
- The canonical `invisible`/`Invisibile` definition registers two reversible, situational effects under the shared GUI context `Contro avversari che non percepiscono l'attaccante`: a stacking generic +2 on `attack.all` and a flat-footed armor-class defense alternative targeting every strike. Without the context, the bonus remains visible but does not alter the ordinary attack total; the defense alternative always carries its applicability label for GUI selection. Detection, localization, and total concealment are reminders, while contextual CMD and Uncanny Dodge interactions remain deferred to their dedicated systems.
- The canonical `negativeLevels`/`Livelli Negativi` definition uses `PerEntry` application: every entry represents exactly one negative level and independently applies stacking penalties of 1 to `attack.all`, `savingThrow.all`, `skill.all`, `abilityCheck.all`, and `combatManeuver.defense.all`, plus a -5 contribution to `hp.max`. BMC inherits its penalty from `attack.all`; initiative and ability-bound checks inherit from `abilityCheck.all`. Each source publishes reminders for level-dependent values, temporary/permanent removal, and the Hit-Dice death threshold. The global `level` target is intentionally not reduced, because doing so would incorrectly recalculate structural level-scaled contributions such as Constitution to maximum hit points.
- The canonical `dying`/`Morente` definition declaratively derives `Privo di Sensi`, whose action inhibition makes the character unable to act without duplicating the restriction, and publishes stabilization and death-threshold reminders. `HitPoints` does not infer Morente from a negative numeric value; the table or a feature-specific component must supply the entry.
- The canonical `dead`/`Morto` definition inhibits every action and reminds that ordinary healing cannot restore life. `HitPoints` creates and latches its source-controlled entry when current hit points reach negative Constitution; ability and effect components may own independent entries when Constitution reaches 0 or an effect kills outright.
- The canonical `nauseated`/`Nauseato` definition inhibits attacks, spells, concentration, standard, round-complete, swift, immediate, free, and attack-replacement actions, while leaving movement and non-actions available. A reminder states the remaining one-move-action-per-turn limit until turn-state tracking can enforce it; it does not numerically reduce movement speed.
- The canonical `paralyzed`/`Paralizzato` definition reversibly installs a final Strength replacement of 0, blocks every movement grant through a global `MovementAdjustment`, and declaratively derives `Indifeso`. Indifeso supplies the final Dexterity replacement of 0 without duplicating it. Its mental-action-only, falling, drowning, and occupied-space rules are reminders. Removing Paralizzato restores Strength and movement and removes only its derived Indifeso source, preserving any independent source that still makes the character helpless.
- The canonical `petrified`/`Pietrificato` definition declaratively derives `Privo di Sensi` and publishes the stone-body integrity and incomplete-restoration rule as a reminder.
- The canonical `unconscious`/`Privo di Sensi` definition declaratively derives `Indifeso`, which supplies the final Dexterity replacement of 0, and inhibits every action. Multiple independent unconscious sources therefore share the helpless effects and removing one source cannot clear the others. `HitPoints` owns a direct source entry only when positive non-lethal damage exceeds current hit points; it does not infer consciousness from negative hit points alone.
- The canonical `prone`/`Prono` definition applies a permanent penalty of 4 to melee attack rolls and exposes two contextual armor-class results: -4 against melee attacks and a stacking generic +4 against ranged attacks. The ordinary armor-class total remains unchanged until the GUI or another caller evaluates the corresponding incoming-attack context. Ranged-weapon restrictions and the action/provocation required to stand are reminders until strike and turn semantics can enforce them.
- The rules condition `Rotto` is intentionally not registered in the character `ConditionManager`: it belongs to individual weapons, armor, shields, tools, wands, and staves, and its behavior depends on the affected object's category. Implement it with the future equipment/item model, where it can alter that object's attacks, damage, critical profile, armor contribution, skill use, charges, repair rules, and value without pretending that the whole character is broken.
- The canonical `bleeding`/`Sanguinante` definition is a `PerEntry` reminder-only condition with the required textual entry parameter `Danno per turno`, allowing values such as `2d6 PF`, `1 Forza`, or an equivalent complete description. Every source publishes a distinct message containing its source, damage, turn timing, Guarire CD 15/magical-healing removal, and non-stacking rule; manual source data survive save/load while messages are recreated. The backend deliberately does not apply damage or resolve which same-kind entry is worst until turn events and typed ability damage/drain exist.
- The canonical `stable`/`Stabilizzato` definition declaratively derives `Privo di Sensi` and publishes hourly recovery and unattended-failure reminders. Stabilization remains an external table or feature decision rather than state owned or inferred by `HitPoints`.
- The canonical `stunned`/`Stordito` definition reversibly applies a penalty of 2 to `armorClass.all`, suppresses the positive ability bonus to armor class, inhibits every action, and reminds the user to drop held items.
- The canonical `pinned`/`Immobilizzato` definition applies and cleans up its separate penalty of 4 to `armorClass.all`, its own armor-class ability suppression, and declaratively derives `Impreparato`. Its independent suppression ensures that a future Schivare Prodigioso exception cannot preserve the armor-class bonus while Immobilizzato. Cleanup removes only the derived entry and therefore preserves independent sources of `Impreparato`. Actions tagged as physical movement, attacks, and the entire Oggetti category are inhibited, while Liberarsi da una lotta remains available; spell-component and Concentration restrictions are a reminder. `In Lotta` and `Immobilizzato` stay independent, with grapple-state transitions owned by their applying component.
